Most AI projects fail at the handoff. So we never leave before it works.

Plenty of teams can call an AI model. Fewer understand what it takes to run automation inside a business that can't afford to break. The hard part was never the model. It was making something an operations team trusts enough to run every day.

So we do it the slow way. One engineer joins your team for about eight weeks. The first two are spent watching and listening, with read-only access to your systems. Then we build inside the tools you already use, and we put a person in front of every decision that carries risk. By the end, your people own it: the code, the prompts, the runbook, and the habits.

No deck. No demo. Working software your staff operate.
