01 / Capabilities

Three systems. Deploy in any order.

Most clients start with one and end up using two. Enablement builds the capability, Automation puts it to work, and the Lab turns what we learn in the field into products.

SYS-01Enablement

AI Enablement

For teams who want to build, not outsource

We stand up the foundation your team needs to use AI safely, then teach them to build on it themselves. Model access, guardrails, evaluation, and the working habits that separate a pilot from a practice.

  • AI workflow audit with a ranked build list
  • Model access, policy, and cost controls
  • Evaluation harnesses for your use cases
  • Hands-on training for engineers and operators
  • Internal tooling and prompt libraries
  • Vendor and platform selection
SYS-02Automation

Automation

For operations that run on queues, inboxes, and documents

We design agents that do the work — routing, drafting, reconciling, escalating — inside your CRM, ticketing, email, and data. Every high-stakes action runs through an approval gate you control, and we stay until it runs without us.

  • Multi-step agents with human approval gates
  • Integration with the systems you already run
  • Document intake, extraction, exception handling
  • Monitoring, logs, and a kill switch on day one
  • Fixed-scope pilots that become managed retainers
  • Code handed over, not held hostage

02 / Deployment

How an engagement runs.

Forward-deployed means we work where the work happens. Short scoping, fast embed, and a handoff that leaves your team in control.

Week 00

Scope the workflow

One working session on the process costing you the most. You get a written scope, a fixed price, and a straight answer on whether AI fits.

Weeks 01–02

Embed with your team

An engineer sits with the people doing the job, maps the real flow including the workarounds, and connects to your systems read-only.

Weeks 03–08

Ship into production

Agents go live behind approval gates. We tune on real volume, widen the gate as trust builds, and instrument everything.

Ongoing

Hand off or retain

Your team gets the repo, the runbook, and the training to operate it. Keep us on a managed retainer or run it yourselves.

04 / Principles

Operators who happen to build AI.

Plenty of teams can call an API. Fewer understand what it takes to run automation inside a business that can't afford to break. That's the gap we work in.

Load path 01

Production, not demos

We ship systems that survive real volume, edge cases, and the messiness of live operations. A slick prototype isn't the goal.

Load path 02

Human-in-the-loop by default

Agents handle the volume; people keep the decisions that carry risk. Approval gates are designed in from the start, not bolted on.

Load path 03

We integrate, we don't replace

Your team keeps the tools they know. We connect AI into the systems you already run instead of forcing a migration nobody asked for.

Load path 04

You own what we build

The code, the prompts, the evals, the runbook. Forward-deployed means we leave your team able to run it without us.
